# V1.2: Product Simplification & Employee-Admin Experience (implemented)

V1.2 supersedes conflicting requirements in [`Spec.md`](Spec.md) and [`ENHANCEMENTS.md`](ENHANCEMENTS.md).

## Retained

* The pure domain engine (`applyAction`), the transition table and the append-only audit.
* The simulated diagnosis, preflight checks (never overridable), timed execution with verification, rollback, similar incidents, SLA indicators and opportunities.
* Metric drill-downs, the CSV export with injection protection, localStorage persistence with safe reset, and the demo mode banner.

## Removed

* Manager role.

* MTTR improvement and financial impact metrics.

* The standalone "How it works" guide.
* Incident correlation and cluster UI.

## Enhanced

* **Approval is mandatory for every AI execution.** It requires acknowledging the action and its risks. It is bound to the incident, device, fix and plan hash, and a changed plan expires it. Duplicate approvals are blocked. The decisions are Approve & Execute, Reject Recommendation and Investigate Manually.
* **Lifecycle.** There are 8 product statuses. The resolution method and the approval status are separate from the status. Overdue is only an SLA flag. A ticket closes only after verification; failed verification leaves it open; rollback is not a resolution.
* **Workspace.** It has 5 KPI tiles. The queue defaults to *All action required*, with filter chips Pending approval, Human investigation and Overdue, and each item explains why it is there.
* **Overview.**
  * Date presets.
  * A KPI row that reconciles to the total.
  * AI-assisted rate = (self-service + human-approved closures) ÷ total.
  * Self-service rate.
  * Unsupported rate (ever unsupported, split into still open versus since resolved).
  * Estimated admin time saved = Σ(baseline − admin minutes), where failed attempts count against it. A tooltip shows the formula.
  * Charts: resolved per day, status distribution, AI versus manual, unsupported trend, turnaround, reopen rate and SLA compliance.

## New

* **Two roles (Employee, IT Admin)** with a role switcher and route guards.
* **Employee portal.**
  * Raise a Ticket.
  * My Tickets, with a status tracker and history.
  * Adaptive guided troubleshooting: one question or step at a time; answers Yes, No, Not sure, Completed, Unable to complete, Issue resolved and Issue still persists; safe steps only.
  * Consent for disruptive fixes.
  * Reopen.
* **Self-service closure.** The ticket auto-closes as Employee Self-Service with approval *Not required*.
* **Escalation on the same incident.** It is triggered by a failed step, the attempt limit (setting), low or network-wide confidence, or an explicit "Get help from IT".
* **Continuous learning.**
  * A manual resolution can be saved as a reusable resolution: pattern, questions, conditions, employee steps, admin steps, outcome, verification, risks and rollback.
  * It starts as *Pending validation*, and an admin validates or rejects it in Opportunities.
  * Validated knowledge adds employee steps to similar tickets (same issue type and shared keywords) and becomes an approvable AI recommendation.
* **AI Settings.**
  * Confidence threshold (default 80%, a prototype assumption).
  * Troubleshooting attempts.
  * Employee consent.
  * Time-saving baselines per issue type, plus review and rollback effort.
  * Approval and verification are locked to *Always on*.
  * Every change is versioned and needs a reason. A threshold change reassesses pending recommendations.
* **Demo scenarios menu:** 1 self-service, 2 escalation and approval, 3/3b unsupported → learning → similar ticket, 4 failed fix with rollback, 5 reopen.
